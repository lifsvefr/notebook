✒️ Welcome to Söguþræðir

A notebook for worldbuilders and long tales — sheets of rag paper, written with glass pens, kept safely in this browser.

✒️ New sheet starts a fresh page.

🧵 Begin lines with #, ## or ### and Threads lets you leap between headings.

💧 Dip the pen to write on in another ink — or select words to highlight them.

📖 Read shows the finished page: colours, headings, and images in their places.

⚙️ Sheets live only in this browser — weave a Backup in Settings now and then.
